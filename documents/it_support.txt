IT Support Guide

Employees can contact the IT Help Desk for laptop, software, network, VPN, and access-related issues.

For critical production issues, employees should contact the IT Help Desk immediately and mark the ticket as high priority.

For password reset requests, employees should use the company's self-service password reset portal.

VPN problems should first be checked by restarting the VPN client and verifying the internet connection.

If the problem continues, employees should create an IT support ticket with a description of the issue and relevant screenshots.
