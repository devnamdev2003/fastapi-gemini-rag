Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leaves per calendar year.

Employees can carry forward a maximum of 10 unused paid leaves to the next calendar year.

Employees must apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance.

For emergency leave, employees should inform their manager as soon as possible.

Employees who join the company during the year receive leave on a prorated basis.

Unused leaves above the maximum carry-forward limit will expire at the end of the calendar year.
